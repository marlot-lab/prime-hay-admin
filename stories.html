Content

A photo of you, your horses or the hay (real stable photography, per the brandbook) makes the first and last story personal. It stays in this browser only.

For the video stories: 10–20 seconds of your own footage (the yard, hay being placed, horses eating) works best. Each story uses a different part of the clip. Without a video, a slow-moving background in brand colours is used. Keep this tab open and visible while a video is being recorded.

Each profile keeps its own rating, review count and review link. Most customers are in the Algarve, so that profile is the default. The location is not shown on the stories.

Google Business Profile → Ask for reviews → copy the link. It becomes the QR code; paste the same link in an Instagram link sticker on the last story.

Reviews

Paste the exact text from Google. Add an English translation if the review is in another language; it is used on the English stories and marked as translated. Leave the reply empty to use a standard thank-you.
