# Prime Hay

## Brand rules
- Website: always use **primehayinternational.com** in customer-facing copy (stories, posts, banners, emails). Never primehayportugal.com.
- House style (from the Pallet Builder launch): deep green `#0C2A02`, gold `#BC993F` / `#D8C081`, cream `#FBFAF6`; Montserrat for headings, Inter for body text; the Prime Hay logo in `stories/img/logo.png`.
- For 3D bales, shavings bags and pallets, reuse the Pallet Builder textures in `stories/img/`.
- Stock: ignore the inventory numbers in Shopify (they are not tracked). Don't flag low or negative stock, and "Now in stock" is fine in copy.
